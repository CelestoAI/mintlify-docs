## Why do you need it?

HR teams spend too much time answering simple questions:

> “How many leaves do I have left?”\
> “What’s the reimbursement process?”\
> “When’s the next holiday?”

These repetitive queries take up over 30% of HR bandwidth and slow response times and employee experience.\
An HR Agent frees your HR team to focus on people, not paperwork.

## What problems does it solve?

| Problem                   | Manual Process          | AI Solution                           |
| :------------------------ | :---------------------- | :------------------------------------ |
| HR FAQs                   | Email or Slack messages | AI answers instantly from policy docs |
| Leave/attendance tracking | Manual HR portal checks | Direct integration with HRMS          |
| Onboarding                | Long email chains       | Guided AI onboarding assistant        |
| Policy updates            | Manual announcements    | Automated notifications               |
| Surveys & engagement      | Manual collection       | AI conducts and summarizes results    |
