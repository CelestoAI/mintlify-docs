## Why do you need an SDR Agent?

If your sales team is spending hours on:

- Finding and enriching leads,
- Writing personalized cold emails,
- Following up manually, and
- Logging data into CRMs

Then you already know how much time is lost before the first real conversation even happens. An SDR Agent automates all this grunt work, giving your team superhuman efficiency so they can focus on closing deals, not chasing inboxes.

## What problems does it solve?

| Problem            | Without AI               | With AI Agent                               |
| :----------------- | :----------------------- | :------------------------------------------ |
| Lead research      | Manual LinkedIn searches | AI scrapes and enriches leads automatically |
| Email outreach     | Hand-written templates   | Personalized cold emails at scale           |
| Follow-ups         | Forgetful humans         | Automated, context-aware follow-ups         |
| Lead qualification | Manual scoring in CRM    | AI qualifies leads based on ICP match       |
| CRM updates        | Tedious manual entry     | AI auto-syncs conversations and updates CRM |
